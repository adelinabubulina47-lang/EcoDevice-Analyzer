Frequently Asked Questions

What is this project? ⌄
EcoDevice Analyzer is an AI-powered platform that helps users decide whether a phone should be recycled or refurbished in order to reduce electronic waste.
Why recycle phones? ⌄
Recycling phones helps reduce pollution, save valuable materials and extend the lifecycle of electronic devices through sustainable solutions.
Who is behind this project? ⌄
This project has been fully developed by two high school students, in the Genius Olympiad Hackathon contest.
